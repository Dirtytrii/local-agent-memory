# Visual & Diagram Aesthetic Anchors

- **High-Contrast Restraint**: Prefer high-contrast monochrome or single-accent palettes over generic blue-purple neon gradients.
- **Real Artifacts First**: Use real terminal captures, clean architecture diagrams, or single-subject visuals instead of cluttered stock collages.
- **No Template Clutter**: Avoid gratuitous pill badges, drop-shadow white cards on every element, and decorative fake metrics.
